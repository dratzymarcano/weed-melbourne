How to Buy Weed Safely in Australia

Protect yourself from scams and ensure quality with our safety checklist

By Mullaways Medical Team • Updated January 2026 • 9 min read

With the growing interest in cannabis in Australia, many people are looking for ways to buy weed safely. Whether you're exploring legal CBD products or considering your options, understanding how to protect yourself from scams and identify quality products is essential.

This guide covers the key safety considerations, red flags to watch for, and how to ensure you're getting quality products from reliable sources.

Legal Disclaimer

Cannabis laws vary by Australian state and territory. THC products remain illegal for recreational use across Australia. This guide is for educational purposes only. Always understand and comply with your local laws before making any purchasing decisions.

Common Cannabis Scams to Avoid

Upfront Payment Scams

Scammers ask for full payment via untraceable methods (cryptocurrency, gift cards, wire transfers) and then disappear without delivering products.

Red Flags:
  • • Only accepting cryptocurrency or gift cards
  • • Asking for "shipping fees" after initial payment
  • • No refund or satisfaction guarantee
  • • Pressure to pay immediately

Fake Social Media Vendors

Instagram, Snapchat, and Telegram are full of accounts claiming to sell cannabis. These are almost always scams or law enforcement operations.

Red Flags:
  • • New accounts with few followers
  • • Stock photos of products
  • • Too-good-to-be-true prices
  • • Aggressive DM solicitation

Contaminated or Fake Products

Some sellers offer products that are synthetic cannabinoids, contaminated with pesticides, or completely fake CBD/THC products.

Red Flags:
  • • Unusually cheap prices
  • • No lab testing or certificates
  • • Packaging that looks unprofessional
  • • Products with no brand or source information

Law Enforcement Operations

Some online cannabis "sellers" are actually law enforcement operations designed to identify and charge buyers.

Red Flags:
  • • Too perfect reputation with no verifiable reviews
  • • Willing to ship anything, anywhere
  • • Asking for personal identifying information
  • • Requesting to meet in person for transactions

How to Identify Quality Cannabis Products

For Flower Products

Quality Indicators

  • • Dense, well-trimmed buds
  • • Vibrant colours (green, purple, orange hairs)
  • • Visible trichomes (crystals)
  • • Strong, pleasant aroma
  • • Slightly sticky texture
  • • No visible seeds or stems

Warning Signs

  • • Brown, dried appearance
  • • Hay or musty smell
  • • Visible mold or mildew
  • • Crumbles to dust
  • • Chemical smell
  • • Suspiciously low price

For Edibles & CBD Products

Quality Indicators

  • • Professional, sealed packaging
  • • Clear dosage information
  • • Ingredients list
  • • Batch numbers for traceability
  • • QR codes linking to lab results
  • • Manufacture and expiry dates

Warning Signs

  • • Homemade-looking packaging
  • • Vague or missing dosage info
  • • No ingredients list
  • • Copied branding from known brands
  • • Unrealistic THC/CBD claims
  • • No way to verify authenticity

For CBD Oil

Quality Indicators

  • • Third-party lab test results available
  • • Clear CBD concentration per ml
  • • Extraction method disclosed (CO2 preferred)
  • • Full ingredient transparency
  • • Type specified (full/broad spectrum, isolate)
  • • Proper glass dropper packaging

Warning Signs

  • • No lab testing available
  • • Vague concentration claims
  • • Unknown extraction method
  • • Medical claims (illegal for CBD)
  • • Unrealistically cheap prices
  • • Plastic packaging

Understanding Lab Test Results (COAs)

A Certificate of Analysis (COA) is a document from an independent laboratory that verifies what's in a cannabis product. Learning to read COAs helps you verify product quality and safety.

What a COA Should Include:

Cannabinoid Profile

Shows the levels of CBD, THC, and other cannabinoids. This should match what's on the product label.

Microbial Testing

Confirms the product is free from harmful bacteria, mold, and other pathogens.

Pesticide Screening

Shows the product is free from harmful pesticides and chemical residues.

Heavy Metal Testing

Confirms absence of lead, mercury, arsenic, and other harmful heavy metals.

Solvent Residue

For extracts, confirms no harmful solvents remain from the extraction process.

Batch Information

Date of testing, batch number, and lab identification should all be present.

Protecting Your Digital Privacy

Use Secure Payment Methods

Prefer payment methods with buyer protection. Be wary of any site that only accepts cryptocurrency or gift cards.

Protect Your Information

Only provide necessary shipping information. Be cautious with any site asking for excessive personal details.

Check Website Security

Ensure the website uses HTTPS (padlock icon). Never enter payment info on unsecured sites.

Avoid Social Media Transactions

Never buy from random social media accounts. These are almost always scams or law enforcement.

Legal Ways to Access Cannabis in Australia

Medical Cannabis

Since 2016, medical cannabis has been legal with a prescription through the TGA's Special Access Scheme or Authorised Prescriber pathway.

  • • Must have a qualifying medical condition
  • • Need a prescription from an authorized doctor
  • • Products dispensed through authorized pharmacies
  • • Fully legal and quality-controlled

Over-the-Counter CBD

Low-dose CBD products (max 150mg/day) are available from pharmacies without prescription since February 2021.

  • • Available from licensed pharmacies
  • • Maximum 150mg CBD per day
  • • Must be Schedule 3 approved products
  • • THC content must be below legal limits
