How Much Does Weed Cost in Australia? 2026 Price Guide

January 10, 2026 6 min read Mullaways Medical Cannabis Team

Quick Answer: In 2026, cannabis prices in Australia typically range from $10-$20 per gram for standard quality, $15-$25 for premium strains, and $200-$400 per ounce. Medical cannabis products range from $100-$400 depending on the product type and THC/CBD content.

Cannabis Pricing Overview in Australia

Understanding cannabis prices in Australia is essential whether you're accessing medical cannabis or curious about market rates. Prices vary significantly based on quality, location, and legality.

The Australian cannabis market has evolved considerably, with medical cannabis becoming more affordable and accessible while street prices remain relatively stable.

Average Prices in 2026

$10-$20 Per Gram (Standard)
$80-$100 Per Quarter (7g)
$150-$180 Per Half (14g)
$250-$400 Per Ounce (28g)

Bulk Buying Tip

Buying larger quantities typically offers significant savings. An ounce often costs 20-40% less per gram than buying individual grams.

Cannabis Prices by Australian State

Prices vary considerably across Australia. Remote areas typically pay more due to supply challenges, while major cities have more competitive pricing.

Sydney / NSW

$12-$18 per gram

Sydney has moderate prices with good availability. Inner west and eastern suburbs tend toward higher prices.

Melbourne / VIC

$10-$16 per gram

Melbourne often has the most competitive prices in Australia due to strong supply networks.

Brisbane / QLD

$12-$20 per gram

$15-$22 per gram

Adelaide prices are moderate, benefiting from proximity to growing regions.

$12-$18 per gram

Hobart / TAS

Is Medical Cannabis Worth It?

While medical cannabis may seem expensive, it offers legal protection, quality assurance, dosing consistency, and potential tax deductions for medical expenses.

Factors Affecting Cannabis Prices

Quality Indicators

  • THC/CBD Content: Higher potency typically commands higher prices
  • Strain Genetics: Popular or rare strains cost more
  • Growing Method: Indoor-grown cannabis is pricier than outdoor
  • Curing Process: Properly cured cannabis sells for premium prices
  • Appearance: Crystal coverage, color, and trim quality affect price

Market Factors

  • Location: Urban areas have more competitive pricing
  • Legality: Medical cannabis has regulated pricing
  • Season: Outdoor harvest times can affect supply
  • Supply Chain: More intermediaries = higher prices

Tips for Getting Good Value

Buy in Bulk

Purchasing larger quantities almost always offers better per-gram pricing.

Consider Medical Access

Medical cannabis is becoming more affordable and offers quality guarantees.

Prioritize Quality

Better quality often means you need less, saving money long-term.

Research Thoroughly

Understanding market rates helps you identify fair pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions About Australian Weed Prices

How much is a 50 bag of weed in Australia?

In Australia, a '50 bag' costs $50 AUD and typically weighs between 2.8 and 3.5 grams of indoor hydro cannabis. In regional or remote areas, a 50 bag may occasionally weigh around 2.5 to 3 grams depending on local supply.

How much is a stick of weed in Australia?

A 'stick' of weed is traditional Australian street slang referring to a small foil packet containing approximately 1.0 to 1.5 grams of cannabis flower, usually sold for $20 to $25 AUD.

How much does an ounce of weed cost in Australia?

In 2026, an ounce of weed (28 grams) in Australia costs between $250 and $380 AUD for standard to top-shelf indoor flower. Street outdoor bush weed can sell for $180–$240/oz, while exotic imported or dispensary-grade craft cultivars can reach $350–$420/oz.

What is a 'Q' of weed in Australia?

A 'Q' stands for a quarter of an ounce, which equals exactly 7 grams of dried cannabis flower. In Australia, a Q typically costs between $80 and $110 AUD.

Is medical cannabis cheaper than street weed in Australia?

Yes, medical cannabis in Australia has dropped significantly in price. Concession and generic scripts now start at $6.50 to $10 per gram ($99 to $140 per 10g-15g tub), making compliant, lab-tested medical cannabis comparable to or cheaper than illicit street weed without the risk of PGR contamination.

Summary

Cannabis prices in Australia range widely based on quality, quantity, and location. In 2026, expect to pay $10-$25 per gram for flower, with bulk purchases offering significant savings. Medical cannabis provides a legal option with prices continuing to become more accessible.

For those seeking legal, quality-assured cannabis products, medical access remains the recommended pathway. Consult with a healthcare provider to explore your options.
