Refunds & Returns

Our commitment to your satisfaction and how we handle returns.

Last updated: February 2026

Contents

Our Satisfaction Guarantee

At Mullaways Medical Cannabis, we stand behind the quality of every product we sell. We're committed to ensuring you receive exactly what you ordered in perfect condition. If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

Quality Promise

Every product is lab-tested and inspected before shipping to ensure it meets our high standards.

Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund or replacement if:

Eligible
  • Product received is different from what you ordered
  • Product arrived damaged or compromised
  • Product quality does not match description
  • Package was lost in transit (with tracking confirmation)
  • Incorrect quantity received
  • Sealed products returned within 14 days of delivery
Not Eligible
  • Products that have been opened or used
  • Change of mind after delivery
  • Products returned after 14 days
  • Dissatisfaction with effects (personal tolerance varies)
  • Incorrect address provided by customer
  • Package not collected from delivery point

Return Process

If you need to request a refund or return, please follow these steps:

1

Contact Our Support Team

Email us via our contact page within 14 days of receiving your order. Include your order number and a description of the issue.

2

Provide Documentation

If applicable, send photos of the product and packaging to help us assess your claim. Clear images help us resolve issues faster.

3

Await Our Response

Our team will review your request within 2 business days and contact you with next steps. We may approve a refund, replacement, or request a return.

4

Return Shipping (If Required)

If a return is required, we'll provide instructions and may cover return shipping for eligible claims. Do not return products without authorisation.

Non-Refundable Items

Due to the nature of cannabis products and health regulations, certain items cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Opened Products: Any product that has been opened, used, or had its seal broken
  • Perishable Items: Edibles and other products with limited shelf life once opened
  • Clearance Items: Products purchased at a discounted clearance price
  • Free Samples: Promotional items included with orders

Damaged or Defective Products

If your product arrives damaged or defective, we take full responsibility. Please:

Document the Damage

Take photos of the packaging and product before handling further.

Contact Us Immediately

Report within 48 hours of delivery for fastest resolution.

Keep All Packaging

Retain original packaging until your claim is resolved.

For damaged or defective products, we will either send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, including any shipping fees paid.

Refund Timeframes

Once your refund is approved:

Bank Transfer 3-5 business days
Bitcoin 1-2 business days
Store Credit Instant

Note: Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Processing times may vary depending on your financial institution.

Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different product, please request a refund and place a new order. This ensures the fastest service and helps us maintain accurate inventory.

Store Credit Option

As an alternative to a refund, you may choose to receive store credit. Store credit is processed instantly and can be used on any future order with no expiration date.

Have questions about returns or need to start a refund request? Our support team is here to help.
