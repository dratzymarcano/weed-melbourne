Common Questions

Bank Transfer FAQ

Everything you need to know about paying with bank transfer.

How do I pay with bank transfer?

At checkout, select "Bank Transfer" and place your order. You'll see our bank details (BSB & account number) and PayID. Open your banking app, send the payment using the exact total shown, and include your order reference number. Then email a screenshot of the transfer to confirm.

What is PayID and how does it work?

PayID is an instant payment system built into all major Australian banking apps. Instead of entering a BSB and account number, you just enter our PayID (email or phone number) and the money arrives within seconds — even on weekends. It's free, instant, and supported by all big four banks.

How long does a bank transfer take?

PayID/Osko transfers are instant (24/7). Standard BSB transfers usually arrive within 1-2 hours during business days. BPAY takes 1-2 business days. We recommend PayID for the fastest experience.

Are there any fees for bank transfer?

No. Bank transfers, PayID, and Osko payments are completely free for both you and us. There are no processing fees, surcharges, or hidden costs. This is why bank transfer is one of the best-value payment options.

Do I need to confirm my payment?

Yes. After transferring the funds, please email a screenshot or receipt of the transfer to our payments team using the Contact page with your order reference. This helps us match your payment and process your order faster.

What if I send the wrong amount?

If you underpay, we'll contact you to arrange the balance. If you overpay, we'll refund the difference to your bank account. Always double-check the exact total from your order confirmation before transferring.

Is bank transfer safe?

Yes. Australian bank transfers are protected by bank-level security and encryption. All transactions are traceable and protected by Australian banking regulations. Unlike cash, there's always a record of your payment.

Which banks support PayID?

All major Australian banks support PayID including Commonwealth Bank, Westpac, ANZ, NAB, ING, Macquarie, Bendigo Bank, Bank of Queensland, Suncorp, and most credit unions. Check your banking app — if you see a "PayID" option, you're good to go.
