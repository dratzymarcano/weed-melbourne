### Low-Competition, High-Search-Volume Target Strategy (Australia / AU Geo-Targeted)

---

## Executive Summary & Market Mechanics

In the Australian digital ecosystem, **Google Ads and paid social channels are heavily restricted or banned outright** for recreational cannabis, medical cannabis promotion (under Therapeutic Goods Administration / TGA guidelines), and nicotine/therapeutic vaping products (post-2024 Therapeutic Goods and Other Legislation Amendment Act). 

Because pay-per-click (PPC) ads are suppressed, **Organic Search (SEO) is the single most valuable customer acquisition channel in Australia**.

### What Defines "Low Competition & Good Search Volume" in This Market?
* **Good Search Volume:** 200 to 5,000+ monthly searches within Australia (`geo:AU`). Highly broad head-terms like "cannabis" (50k+/mo) are dominated by government portals (TGA, ADF, Healthdirect). In contrast, localized and product-specific terms carry immense commercial intent with zero traffic wastage.
* **Low Competition (KD 5–32 on 0–100 scale):** Search engine results pages (SERPs) characterized by weak forum threads, outdated blog posts, or thin landing pages. These can be outranked rapidly using targeted landing pages, programmatic city templates, and comprehensive guides with proper internal linking and schema markup.
* **Zero-Click Resistance:** Transactional, pricing, and localized queries require clicking through to check availability, menus, or delivery details.
